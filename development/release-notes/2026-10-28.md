# Release Notes: 28 October 2026

## 1. Rotations, Moving Frames and Their Holonomy; the Two-Body Problem

In this update physis carries coordinates between frames that move, and propagates orbits ([decision 0009](../decisions/0009-rotations-frames-and-the-two-body-problem.md)).

- **Rotations** in every form: unit quaternions, matrices (Shepperd's method both ways, stable near 0 and π), axis and angle, rotation vectors through the exponential map, and Euler angles of all twelve sequences, intrinsic or extrinsic, with their singular angles handled. A rotation gives its SU(2) element in `symmetry`'s convention, so the double cover is one object.
- **Moving frames.** A rigid motion with its rates carries positions and velocities, and to second order accelerations, from one frame to another; motions compose and invert, and two motions are compared by Chasles's screw, an angle and a slide that do not depend on the frames chosen.
- **The frame graph.** A world's frames form a tree of motions that depend on time; the motion between any two is composed through their lowest common ancestor. A second route between two frames closes a loop, and its holonomy, the motion once round it, measures how far the two routes disagree. The navigation domain builds the Solar System's catalogue of frames on it.
- **The two-body problem** in `mechanics`, the module's first content: classical elements from a state and back (with the angles that replace undefined ones for circular and equatorial orbits), Kepler's equation for every conic, propagation by universal variables on any conic, and the frames of an orbit (RTN, LVLH, VNC and the perifocal frame) with their rates.

The checks hold each conversion to its round trip and to its singular cases, the double cover to `symmetry`, the motions' rates to finite differences, holonomies to loops constructed with a known twist; the elements to Vallado's own code, and propagation to calx's integration of the equations of motion over a day, with energy and angular momentum kept.
