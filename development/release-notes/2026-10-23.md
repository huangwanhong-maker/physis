# Release Notes: 23 October 2026

## 1. The Open Qubit

In this update physis begins its quantum theory ([quantum](../../quantum/README.md)) with the open two-level system: a driven qubit that decays by emitting photons. Its master equation is integrated exactly on the Bloch vector, and its quantum trajectories, between and at their jumps, in closed form. The two descriptions are checked against each other: the mean time between photons of a trajectory is the inverse of the master equation's emission rate, to fifteen digits.

## 2. Events Counted Once

In this update a dynamical system can declare a silent jump, a change of state after each step that emits no event. With it an event can be an upward crossing counted once, as the broad spikes of conductance-based neurons need.

## 3. Units and Dynamical Systems

In this update physis has its first C++ ([ADR 0004](../decisions/0004-the-continuum-units-and-dynamics.md)):

- **Units.** Quantities can be read with their units, such as "120 mS/cm^2" or "87.8 pA", and a quantity of the wrong dimension is refused.
- **Dynamical systems.** A system can be stated once, as a flow with events, jumps and refractory dwells, and then integrated. Linear systems are integrated exactly, and stay accurate even when their components differ by ten orders of magnitude.

The plan now also covers analysis, control, computational geometry, quantum theory and statistical mechanics, so that quantum neurons have a home when they come.
