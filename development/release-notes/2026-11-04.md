# 2026-11-04

## Chains on a Classified Set, and the Probability of a Record of Events

In this update physis can say how long each level of a classification is remembered, and give the exact probability of a record of events whose cells change between them ([decision 0020](../decisions/0020-chains-on-a-classified-set-and-the-probability-of-a-record.md); the infrastructure's [ADR 0067](https://github.com/huangwanhong-maker/The-Science-Infrastructures/blob/main/development/decisions/0067-event-spacetime.md)).

- **Chains on a classified set.** States classified at nested levels (divisions, groups, classes), each level redrawn from a law at its own rate. Its modes are the functions of each level with mean zero on the level above, so that no level is remembered longer than a coarser one, and its kernel is closed. The closed form agrees with the matrix exponential, and the expected changes between two observations with Van Loan's integral.
- **Its rates fitted.** The rates' maximum likelihood from observed transitions, by Newton's method with Laplace's errors. A rate the data drive to zero is reported at its bound, and the fit converges when a further step would gain less than 10⁻⁹ in the log-likelihood. Rates simulated from a known chain are recovered.
- **The probability of a record.** Cells with finite states and propagators, joined in the order of time by pieces (laws, sums, transitions, observations, rules over several cells), are contracted exactly, with log Z returned. Slicing time changes nothing, and a record with no observation has Z = 1, as the checks confirm against enumeration.
- **First use.** The infrastructure's urban-science domain fits how long Melbourne's buildings keep their uses and its premises their trades, census by census from 2002 to 2024, and contracts the buildings' chains on the district's event complex.

## Triangle Surfaces in Space: Their Sections, the Solids They Bound, and Close Pairs of Points

In this update physis can measure a mesh as a solid, and cut it by a plane exactly ([decision 0019](../decisions/0019-triangle-surfaces-their-sections-and-solids.md); the infrastructure's [ADR 0066](https://github.com/huangwanhong-maker/The-Science-Infrastructures/blob/main/development/decisions/0066-the-anatomy-domain-and-the-body-atlas.md)).

- **Solids.** A closed triangle surface, oriented outward, gives its volume, centre and inertia, as `MassProperties` that compose with boxes and point masses. A box of twelve triangles is the box to rounding, and a regular tetrahedron has the inertia m a²/20 about every axis.
- **Sections.** A plane cuts a surface into oriented loops: counter-clockwise round each piece and clockwise round its holes. Their areas add to the area of the cut. The loops are joined without a tolerance, and stay exact where a corner lies on the plane.
- **Cavalieri's principle, checked.** The sections of a sphere of 5,120 triangles and of a torus integrate to their volumes to 10⁻⁷, joining the two computations.
- **What touches what.** Of points carrying labels, the pairs of labels that come within a distance, with their least distance. Points are welded where a mesh repeats them.
- **First use.** The infrastructure's anatomy domain measures each of BodyParts3D's 2,234 parts of the body with these functions and cuts the body in the planes of imaging.
