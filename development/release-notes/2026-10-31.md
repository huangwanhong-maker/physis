# 2026-10-31

## Kinematic Waves, Master Equations, and Reaction–Diffusion on Graphs

In this update physis carries crowds, walkers and species on graphs ([decision 0016](../decisions/0016-kinematic-waves-master-equations-and-reaction-diffusion.md); the infrastructure's [ADR 0060](https://github.com/huangwanhong-maker/The-Science-Infrastructures/blob/main/development/decisions/0060-streets-as-open-systems.md)). Kinematic waves run on networks of links joined at nodes, by Godunov's scheme on the Lighthill–Whitham–Richards law, with entries, exits, junctions of turning fractions (first in, first out or not) and laws scheduled in time; the crowd is conserved to rounding, and a red light passes nobody. A scalar is advected and diffused on a graph in flux form. A new module, `statistical_mechanics`, holds Markov jump processes: their stationary laws, relaxation, entropy production and cycle affinities with Kolmogorov's criterion, first passage and phase-type laws, and the entropy of mixing. `chemistry` gains reaction–diffusion on graphs, Turing's condition and the pinning of fronts on a chain. The urban-science domain draws on all of them: the waves of a crowd on Swanston Street, the circulation of a district, a store's visitors spreading along its street.

## Bodies with Joints, Random Fields Read Anywhere, and Bodies in a Fluid

In this update physis moves bodies made of jointed links. A planar tree of rigid links, on a fixed or a floating base, is stated by its Lagrangian and moved by the discrete action's variational integrator, or by Newton–Euler's equations with semi-implicit Euler or Runge–Kutta; the two forms of its equations agree to 1.9 × 10⁻¹⁶. Its feet meet the ground by compliant contact: Hunt and Crossley's normal force and regularised Coulomb friction ([decision 0014](../decisions/0014-articulated-bodies-in-the-plane-and-compliant-contact.md)). The robotics domain's rabbit and dog are built on them.

A stationary Gaussian random field of squared-exponential covariance can now be read at any point, in any dimension, by the spectral representation, the same everywhere for a given seed and stream. The world's presumed properties of a place, a ground's friction or the ocean's density, are realised this way ([decision 0015](../decisions/0015-stationary-random-fields.md)).

The Euler–Lagrange equations of any Lagrangian are solved for the acceleration by calx's jets, so that any body stated by its Lagrangian can be moved by the explicit methods too. On it stands a tree of slender links in a fluid by strip theory: the water's kinetic energy, its added mass, in the Lagrangian; its drag a force; a current carried in ([decision 0017](../decisions/0017-bodies-in-a-fluid-and-lagrangian-dynamics.md)). The robotics domain's fish swims in it. The theory is in the note on discrete mechanics, §§6–8.
