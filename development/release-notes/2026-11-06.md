# 2026-11-06

## The Laws beneath Physical Computing

In this update physis gains the general laws that computing with matter rests on: how flows lose and keep information, where they change their behaviour, and how water, heat and reacting chemicals move ([decision 0022](../decisions/0022-the-laws-beneath-physical-computing.md); the infrastructure's [ADR 0076](https://github.com/huangwanhong-maker/The-Science-Infrastructures/blob/main/development/decisions/0076-physical-computing.md)). Each law is held to a closed form or a published value, in 124 checks; the whole suite passes, 1176 checks.

- **Chaos and stability.** Flows and maps of any dimension report their Lyapunov spectra and Kaplan–Yorke dimension. Lorenz's spectrum sums to −41/3 and its largest exponent is 0.9056; Hénon's and the logistic map's are exact. Periodic flows give their Floquet multipliers, and the Mathieu tongue appears where theory puts it.
- **Where behaviour changes.** Branches of equilibria are followed past their folds by pseudo-arclength continuation, and their folds and Hopf points are located. The cusp, the Brusselator's b = 1 + a² and a predator–prey's Hopf point in closed form are found. Kuramoto's oscillators lock at the coupling theory predicts.
- **Water and heat.**
  - **Waves:** gravity–capillary waves in a basin with walls, their energy kept by the leapfrog step.
  - **Convection:** the onset of convection between plates found by shooting: 1707.762, 1100.65 and 27π⁴/4 for the three pairs of boundaries.
  - **Two-layer flow:** quasi-geostrophic flow in a channel, growing at Phillips's rates and keeping its energy and enstrophy.
  - **Capillarity:** Young–Laplace, Hagen–Poiseuille and Washburn's law.
- **Networks and fluctuations.**
  - **Kirchhoff networks** of conductances on any graph.
  - **The Potts model,** sampled and checked against exact enumeration.
  - **Fluctuations of currents** from a chain's tilted generator, with the thermodynamic uncertainty relation and Landauer's bound as the floors of a computation's cost. The SI's seven defining constants are now in `units`.
- **Stiff chemistry in space.**
  - **Any kinetics, cell by cell,** on a graph, a torus or a dish with no-flux walls.
  - **A split step implicit in the reactions,** so that the Belousov–Zhabotinsky reaction runs at the steps diffusion allows. Its Newton iteration is damped and kept to non-negative concentrations, because the Oregonator's kinetics have a pole that plain Newton crosses.
  - **Polynomial ODEs compiled exactly** to networks of mass action on dual rails, Lorenz's among them.
- **Found on the way.**
  - A periodic box is not a channel: it grows modes that walls forbid.
  - A Fisher front reaches its speed only logarithmically, so it is checked against Bramson's correction.
  - On a torus a plane wave can circulate and mimic a spiral; a dish's walls tell them apart.
