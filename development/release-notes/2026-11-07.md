# 2026-11-07

## H3, the Hexagonal Grid of the Earth

In this update physis holds H3 ([decision 0023](../decisions/0023-h3.md)): a place's cell at any resolution, a cell's centre, boundary, parent and area, pentagons and their text form, ported from Uber's reference implementation (Apache-2.0, whose notice its tables keep) and held to h3-py on a fixture of cells, parents and boundaries. Anything known of a place can now be moved onto one grid; the Urban Science Workbench draws the world's people and its economies on it.
