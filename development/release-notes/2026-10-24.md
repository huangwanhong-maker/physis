# Release Notes: 24 October 2026

## 1. Cochains, the Hodge Decomposition and the Sphere

In this update physis can do discrete exterior calculus on any of morphe's complexes, and triangulate points on the sphere ([decision 0008](../decisions/0008-cochains-hodge-and-the-sphere.md)).

- **Cochains.** A value on each edge can be integrated round any loop. It can be tested for being a gradient, which yields its potential. It can be split into a gradient, a curl round the faces and a harmonic part round the holes, with weights on the edges and faces.
- **Large complexes.** Small complexes are decomposed exactly, large ones by conjugate gradients, and the two agree.
- **Flows.** A flow on a network can be split with a conductance on each edge, by Ohm's and Kirchhoff's laws: into the flow its balances drive through the conductances, the circulation round each face and the circulation round the holes. Large and small links each carry their share.
- **The sphere.** Places on the sphere are triangulated into their spherical Delaunay triangles: the faces of a map of places, robust to points that share a circle. Great-circle angles and spherical areas come with them.

The infrastructure's economics domain uses both for the circulation of value between the world's economies and across time.

## 2. ℤ₂ Gauge Theories Computed Exactly

In this update physis computes a ℤ₂ lattice gauge theory on any 2-complex exactly, not only on closed surfaces and discs ([decision 0007](../decisions/0007-exact-z2-contraction-and-finite-heat-bath.md)).

- **Exact computation.** Each 2-cell may carry its own coupling, and any links may be fixed: a gauge, a boundary, an observation. The contraction sums the free links along an order of the 2-cells. It returns log Z, the mean of every link and plaquette, two-point functions, the law of products at a cut, and the spin networks along which the theory's surfaces cross it, each with its weight. One pass over calx's jets gives the gradient and the Hessian of log Z in the couplings, exactly.
- **Monte Carlo.** Gauge fields take the same couplings and frozen links. A new heat bath for every finite group draws each link in proportion to its exact weight. It also mixes where ℤ₂'s Metropolis cannot: Metropolis is periodic on links the weight does not see.
- **SU(2).** The recoupling theory counts the invariants of any tensor product of spins exactly.

The infrastructure's history domain uses all of it for the spin foam of a history, instead of code of its own.
