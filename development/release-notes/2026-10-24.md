# Release Notes: 24 October 2026

## 1. ℤ₂ Gauge Theories Computed Exactly

In this update physis computes a ℤ₂ lattice gauge theory on any 2-complex exactly, not only on closed surfaces and discs ([decision 0007](../decisions/0007-exact-z2-contraction-and-finite-heat-bath.md)).

- **Exact computation.** Each 2-cell may carry its own coupling, and any links may be fixed: a gauge, a boundary, an observation. The contraction sums the free links along an order of the 2-cells. It returns log Z, the mean of every link and plaquette, two-point functions, the law of products at a cut, and the spin networks along which the theory's surfaces cross it, each with its weight. One pass over calx's jets gives the gradient and the Hessian of log Z in the couplings, exactly.
- **Monte Carlo.** Gauge fields take the same couplings and frozen links. A new heat bath for every finite group draws each link in proportion to its exact weight. It also mixes where ℤ₂'s Metropolis cannot: Metropolis is periodic on links the weight does not see.
- **SU(2).** The recoupling theory counts the invariants of any tensor product of spins exactly.

The infrastructure's history domain uses all of it for the spin foam of a history, instead of code of its own.
